Author Page

"I write the stories I needed to read — stories that feel like Ghana, that breathe Accra's air, that know what it is to stand between two worlds."

Kofi Bismark is a Ghanaian author whose work moves between urban fantasy, literary historical fiction, and self-help. His stories are rooted in Accra — in the mythology, the history, and the daily texture of a city that contains multitudes.

His debut trilogy, Accra Between, draws on Ga threshold mythology to build an urban fantasy world unlike anything set on the African continent. His historical novel The Last Harmattan brings the Gold Coast independence era to life through intimate, close-range fiction. And Wind Them Up offers something more personal: eight life lessons shaped by lived experience and shared without pretence.

All three books are available worldwide on Amazon.
