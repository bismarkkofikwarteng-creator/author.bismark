"I write the stories I needed to read. Stories that feel like Ghana, that breathe Accra's air."

Kofi Bismark is a Ghanaian author writing stories rooted in African history, culture, and the human spirit. His work moves across urban fantasy, literary historical fiction, behavioral guides, and self-help. All of it is rooted in Accra, in the mythology, the history, and the daily texture of a city that contains multitudes.

His debut trilogy, Accra Between, draws on Ga threshold mythology to build a supernatural world unlike anything set on the African continent. His historical novel The Last Harmattan brings the Gold Coast independence era to life through intimate, close-range fiction set between 1947 and 1957.

Wind Them Up offers something more personal: eight life lessons shaped by years in classrooms, shared without pretence. And The Compulsive Masturbation Recovery System delivers a clinical, structured behavioral recovery framework for those who need it. All four books are available worldwide on Amazon.
